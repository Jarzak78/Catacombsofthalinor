# Project guidance

The project owner wants licensing and intellectual-property concerns
raised when features or content are suggested. Read `legal/README.md`
before introducing outside content or changing release packaging.

- Prefer project-created content or sources whose licenses cover the
  intended use. Record the source, creator, version, permission/license,
  required credit, and modifications for imported assets or code.
- Preserve `CREDITS.txt`, `legal/CC-BY-4.0.txt`, modification notices, and
  all bundled dependency notices in source and executable distributions.
  SRD permission covers the credited SRD material, not every related
  book, setting, character, illustration, or trademark.
- Flag specific concerns and their uncertainty promptly, and suggest an
  original or appropriately licensed alternative. Do not incorporate
  outside material whose permission cannot be established; continue
  unrelated authorized work. This is not a blanket approval requirement
  for routine project-created or properly licensed changes.
- Do not promise legal clearance, assign exclusive copyright to generated
  output, or select a new whole-project license without the owner's
  direction. Keep project-specific material distinct from third-party
  licensed material and avoid official-branding or endorsement claims.
