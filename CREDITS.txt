CHANGES TO REFERENCED MATERIAL

Selected class features, spells, equipment profiles, and combat concepts
are adapted and simplified for a solo computer game. SRD 5.2 was the
project's original cited reference; shared material was also reviewed
against SRD 5.2.1. This is not a complete implementation of either SRD.

Changes include a room-based campaign, immediate death at zero HP,
hidden-only Sneak Attack, DC 20 hiding, opening encounter and ambush
rules, humorous critical-failure self-damage, five armor slots, capped
combined armor enchantments, randomized loot, altered prices, and
custom magical buffs and supplies. Descriptions and instructions have
been rewritten for this game's interface. The campaign's setting,
characters, lore, monster roster, and treasure probabilities were
developed for this project rather than copied from an SRD adventure.
